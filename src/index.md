#### The Name

The name was inspired by the [lyrics of a death 'n' roll](https://genius.com/Gorefest-soul-survivor-lyrics) song. Brining the idea of Heart and Soul and our human attributes was a nice contrast to the technology I was working with on a day-to-day basis – the Machine. I first used the name when I set up on [Wordpress.com](https://timklapdor.wordpress.com/) back in 2011 to record an upcoming trip to the US and that has now evolved into this space.
