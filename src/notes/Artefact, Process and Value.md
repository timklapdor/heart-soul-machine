#### The Artefact/Process Tension

Every act of production involves both an **artefact** (the output, the end product, the thing) and a **process** (the method, the craft, the system through which the thing is made). These are not the same, and the **value** we assign to each is rarely in proportion to what each actually contributes.

**The core propositions:**
- A tool or method that produces an artefact quickly is not inherently more valuable than one that produces it slowly, if the process carries value beyond the output
- Artefacts can be identical while the processes that produced them are radically different – and those differences matter, even when they’re invisible in the artefact itself
- When we value only the artefact, we systematically undervalue or destroy the processes that sustain the systems around production: ecosystems, communities, livelihoods, craft knowledge

**The role of context and cost:**
- Monetary value is one register of value, not the only one
- A process that cares for the world (workers, environment, community) has value that dollar cost doesn’t fully capture
- Competitive cost pressure tends to drive artefact-focus and process-erasure, because process costs are visible and artefact costs are not

**The duality:**
- Artefact and process are not opposites – they are linked, and the value of each is partly determined by the other
- The right relationship between them is dynamic, not fixed: context determines when process deserves more emphasis and when artefact does
- The pathology is getting stuck – either ignoring process entirely (artefact-only thinking) or fetishising process at the expense of any output

**Applications:**
- Design: craft is in the process; clients want the artefact; AI accelerates the tension
- Food production: factory farming vs. regenerative agriculture produce similar artefacts through radically different processes
- Education: learning is a process; credentials are artefacts; optimising for credentials at the expense of learning is a classic example of artefact-over-process failure
- AI-generated content: the artefact exists; the process of understanding, deciding, and crafting is absent

**The key question to ask of any production system:** _What is being lost in the process that doesn’t show up in the artefact?_
